What Beyond Code is

A room where tech people swap what actually works

Nigerian tech has plenty of events, but too few where people trade real value. Beyond Code fixes that with practical sessions, real numbers and honest feedback, all in one focused day.

More about the event

Four practical sessions

Follow one product from its first customer to its first investor: the market, the sale, the money and a live Pitch Pit.

The Pitch Pit

Five founders pitch for 3 minutes each and get 3 minutes of direct feedback from the investor, live in front of the room.

Networking that works

Role coloured badges and the Ask Board help you find the right person in minutes, then investor office hours keep the conversation going.
