The programme

Saturday, session by session

Four sessions on what happens after the code: perception, building with AI, communication and ethics, and a closing panel.

Date

Saturday 27 March 2027

Time

11:00am to 3:00pm

Venue

Ibadan, Oyo State, venue to be confirmed

The sessions

Four sessions, one honest day

01

Opening talk and live audit

Perception Marketing: How Nigerians Decide to Trust and Buy

How people see your product decides whether they trust it, pay for it and recommend it. Positioning, pricing in naira, brand signals and social proof, then a live audit of three attendee products.

02

Live build for engineers

The Build: Shipping Faster with AI

A working feature built live with AI coding tools, from a problem the room votes on. Where AI saves hours, where it gets things wrong, and building for patchy networks and local payments.

03

Practical clinic

Communication and Ethics: Doing Business People Trust

Proposals and messages that get replies, honest expectations on price and timelines, and the hard calls on customer data and money.

04

Closing panel

The Honest Panel: Building, Selling and Surviving in Nigerian Tech

Four practitioners from different corners of tech talk openly about what really happens beyond the code, with questions from the room.
