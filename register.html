FAQ

Quick answers

Who can attend?+

Anyone who works in or near tech: founders, engineers, product people, sales and marketing, students and investors.

How many seats are there?+

100. When they are gone, they are gone.

What time does it start and end?+

Doors open for a 10:00am start and the day closes at 2:00pm on Saturday 27 March 2027.

Where is it?+

The venue is still to be confirmed. Everyone who registers hears first.

What is the Ask Board?+

A board where you post one ask and one offer. You will meet people who can help with yours and need yours.

How does the raffle work?+

One laptop is drawn live at the close. Every ticket includes one free raffle entry.

What are the role coloured badges?+

Your badge colour shows your role, so you can find the right person in seconds.
