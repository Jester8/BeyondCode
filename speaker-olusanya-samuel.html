About Olusanya

From the first idea to production.

Olusanya Samuel A. is a full-stack software developer based in Ibadan, Nigeria, with more than five years of professional experience. He builds responsive, scalable web and mobile applications and enjoys carrying a product from an early idea through implementation and into production.

His approach brings together clean architecture, maintainable code and careful attention to user experience, visual detail and performance. He has worked with different teams and organizations to build and ship software, and is especially interested in the decisions that make a product useful, reliable and ready to grow.

Olusanya has a background in Computer Engineering and is pursuing postgraduate studies in Software Engineering. He also shares what he learns with the tech community. Through Beyond Code, he hosts conversations about the full product journey: understanding a market, building and selling a product, finding investment and turning technical work into practical value.

His public project work has included Agrochain, an agri-tech initiative exploring secure digital records for farmers and better access to markets, credit, insurance and export opportunities.
